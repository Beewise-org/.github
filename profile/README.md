# 👋 Welcome to Beewise

> Empowering modern beekeeping through IoT, telemetry, and intelligent management.

## 🐝 About Us
Beewise is a smart beekeeping platform designed to help apiarists and commercial beekeepers digitally monitor and manage their apiaries and beehives in real-time. By combining cutting-edge IoT hardware telemetry with cloud intelligence, we bring data-driven insights to sustainable beekeeping.

## 🚀 What We Do
* **Real-Time Telemetry:** Continuous tracking of hive temperature, humidity, weight fluctuations, and battery health.
* **Apiary Management:** Digital tools to organize physical locations, hive distributions, and operational workflows.
* **Alerts:** Instant notifications and automated messaging channels to prevent colony losses and optimize honey production.

## 🔒 Private Organization
Most of our repositories, source code, and internal infrastructure pipelines are private and restricted to the Beewise engineering team.
